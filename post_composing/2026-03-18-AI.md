AI math research is real and accelerating fast, but the systems behind the breakthroughs (AlphaGeometry, AlphaProof) are not publicly available. The only one you can actually use is [Gemini Deep Think](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/gemini-3/#note-from-ceo), locked behind Google's $250/month Ultra plan.

For everyday AI access, the free tiers today are genuinely strong. Google's free Gemini Flash, OpenAI's free GPT-5, and Anthropic's free Sonnet 4.6 all handle coding, writing, and general reasoning very well. Paying for a premium tier is only worth it if you regularly tackle expert-level scientific reasoning or complex multi-step research tasks — otherwise the free tier covers most needs.
