> As a graduate student, there is always some day occuring when I realize it necessary to take deeper part in seminars. Then the question comes out, how can I get the best of it? 

##### 目标：提取内容

1. 为了解决什么问题？——迷失时的锚点。细节不重要。

2. 注意自己何时跟不上、为什么 ——往往意味着background不足或重要内容。

3. 找出核心论断 ——其余都是铺垫或佐证。


##### **当前状态：** 

到场听，记下理解与困惑——积累曝光、模式识别，积累知识。

**阻力与对策：**

1. **事后跟进不了了之** 

"整理笔记、解答疑问"太模糊，没法开始。拆成两步：

- 讲座结束马上挑一个困惑，写成清晰问题。
- 每周固定时间：专门尝试解决它。

2. **讲座中难以提问** 

节奏太快 + 问题感觉太基础。对策：

- 讲座中：只标记短语，不试图写完整问题。
- 提问环节："您能对X再多说一点吗？"就够了。
- 讲座后：找讲者问"X有什么好的参考资料？"

##### **下一步：** 每一步顺理成章了，再推进。

总结一下，我现状是记所有笔记，有问题也标出来。但是我的问题没法问，记下笔记也没重点。如此，

1. 听之前，看下摘要，提取main problem，
2. 每次听的时候，
   1. 记住main problem他们要解决的， 然后围绕这个听。
   2. 如果走神了记下来，当场想想为啥，Q&A问一下，结束后想想答案。
3. 每次Seminar的时候，尽量与speaker互动一下：
   1. 在中间要求他解释某概念
   2. 结束后掺乎一下问个问题，或者问问有啥reference可以看
   3. 如果感兴趣speaker，夸一下，介绍一下自己（social一下）
4. Seminar之后，用碎片时间回忆讲了啥，甚至给别人说下，交流下问题。记手机里，有空整理到文档里甚至看看原文章。
   1. Main probelm
   2. Their claim or result
   3. 还有什么主要的问题。
5. 闲的时候，整理一下之前的Seminar们。名词解释，内容疑惑，研究相关，内容质疑。

Previous thoughts

I am going to join a lot of seminars this year. However, to get not the most, but at least some of it, I plan to learn something from each talk. 

- Remember the main idea before the talk, read the title carefully, and refer to the abstracts if possible.
- Note down what I hear to my notebook, try not missing important things. Pay attention to the figures even grab them into the notebooks. 
- Ask questions during and after the talk, make sure you understand. 
  - Start from asking one question after the talk. See it as a task. 
  - If too ashamed to ask any, like the case I don't know the field at all, try searching it during the talk. 
- Give a one-sentence summary to the talk. 
- Pictures of imporant result.
- Give explanations to one new notion (maybe find someone else to explain it to you).
- Note down my question and answer.

 EFT around us 3.17

- Summary: SM is not enough, but experiments limited by energy scale, so we use EFT. For Neutrinal mass, we integrate out RH neutrino (which has mass > $TeV$) in seesaw, and test the SMEFT. For GR, it should be EFT since it's not renormalizable. Consider GR, expand with derivatively so we work in semi-classical regime. WE find 0th derivative force CC small, 2nd derivative is ok so we have low-ernergy GR, $\mathcal G_{ab}$ is important so people should consider more than 1 scalar. 
- Notion: mass dimension of EFT. Consider the low energy theory, assume coupling $g$ has mass dimension $-p$, then the EFT contribution (integration out of heavier fields) will yield $g_{eff} = c_1/M_1^{p}+c_2/M_2^p + ...$ If $p>0$ then smallest $M$ wins (like $m_e$), if $p<0$ then largest $M$ wins (like $M_p$). Positive-dimension couplings ($p<0$) will have corrections from large mass, if the renormalized value is small it yields naturalness problem. In SM, they are CC problem (dimension 4) and Higgs hierarchy problem (dimension 2). 
- Question: Does it mean all positive dimension couplings shouldn't exist somehow?
