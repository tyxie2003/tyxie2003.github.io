2026-02-10

Good email writing: **clarity, tone control, and structure**, not fancy vocabulary.

Use templates in mind:

- Context (1 sentence) 

*Following our discussion today…*
*I’ve been thinking about the CS equation…*

- main point (2-4) 

*It seems to me that…*
*I’m wondering whether…*

Expressing an idea politely

> *It seems to me that…*
> *I was under the impression that…*
> *If I understand correctly…*

Disagreeing gently

> *I may be mistaken, but…*
> *I wonder if…*
> *One possible issue might be…*

Inviting discussion

> *I’d be interested to hear your thoughts.*
> *We can discuss this further…*

- soften/collaboration signal (1)

*I may be misunderstanding something.*
*I’d appreciate your thoughts.*
*Happy to discuss tomorrow.*
